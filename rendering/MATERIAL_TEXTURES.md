# Generated material swatches

Built-in image generation created six photographic material textures. Each prompt requested a square, close-up material image filling the frame, without a border, scene, text, sphere or watermark. The site draws circular crops and spherical lighting in CSS.

- Sand: original warm ivory/sand bouclé upholstery with dense wool loops, curled fibers and realistic fine relief, evenly lit overhead.
- Rose: dusty warm rose woven upholstery, tiny interlaced threads, fine fibers and an earthy muted blush palette, evenly lit overhead.
- Leather: rich cognac premium button-tufted leather, fine natural grain, small upholstered buttons, curved stitched diamond seams and realistic folds, without excessive shine.
- Greige: warm greige/taupe-gray woven upholstery, tight interlaced yarn, subtle fibers and a natural gray-brown palette, evenly lit overhead.
- Charcoal: deep charcoal/olive-black woven upholstery, tiny dense threads, tactile fibers and warm undertones, evenly lit overhead.
- Satin metal: neutral gray satin powder-coated paint, extremely fine micrograin and subtle metallic flecks, evenly lit without large brushed streaks. CSS blends this texture with the bicycle and car paint colors.

Assets: `public/assets/material-textures/{sand,rose,leather,greige,charcoal,satin-metal}.webp` (512px, WebP Q88), and matching `*-small.webp` files (128px, WebP Q88) used by the circular controls. All six small swatches total 32,466 bytes. The larger files are used by the material-detail cards. Original model render sequences are unchanged.
